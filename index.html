About

Henrry Alberty Carrasco Dueñas is a Peruvian student at Innova Schools – Nuevo Chimbote, graduating in December 2026. He was born on June 13, 2009, in San Miguel, Santiago de Chile, and lives in Nuevo Chimbote, in the Áncash region of Peru.

In 2026 he wrote an original research paper on the Navier–Stokes equations — the subject of one of the Clay Mathematics Institute’s Millennium Prize Problems — and placed 18th in the International Academic Research Competition (IARCO), becoming the first Peruvian and South American to advance this far in the competition.

Outside the classroom he reactivated the Leo Club Nuevo Chimbote (Lions Clubs International) as its president, created the free education program “El Arte de Pensar” for children from low-income families, founded the social platform REU, and published the poetry collection “Entropía del Amor” on Amazon Kindle.
